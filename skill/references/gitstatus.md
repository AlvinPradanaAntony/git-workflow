# gitstatus

Read `references/common.md` before acting. Follow the user's explicit scope and this command's mutation boundary.

Inspect branch/HEAD, operation state, staged, unstaged, untracked, conflicts, upstream and known ahead/behind state. Do not fetch. Label remote knowledge as cached when appropriate. Show counts and relevant filenames, distinguish partially staged files and submodule changes, then suggest an appropriate next command. No changes means say the working tree is clean, not invent a commit.
